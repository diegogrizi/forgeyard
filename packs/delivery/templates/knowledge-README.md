# Project knowledge

Store concise, durable facts that future sessions need to continue safely. Prefer links to executable code, tests, decisions, and receipts over copied chat transcripts.

Record:

- confirmed product and technical constraints;
- commands that were actually exercised and their environment;
- non-obvious failure modes and recovery steps;
- links to accepted decisions and current evidence.

Do not store credentials, tokens, private conversation dumps, personal data, or claims that were not verified. When a fact changes, amend it with a date and the evidence that supersedes it.
